Protocol Lab

Check protocol provisions on declared cases and repeat every result

What this is

Choose a question, set the conditions, run a real computation in your browser and get a report that explains every result. Your inputs stay on your device; nothing is sent to the server.

  • Compliance — are the checked provisions met in this experiment?
  • Model validity — which data and assumptions link the model to a real system? Shown separately as hypotheses.
  • Readiness for use — never granted here: results are research outputs (operational_use: false), not an admission under DKP-8-SIMULATION-001.
